# Project governance

RunSigil uses a maintainer-led, evidence-oriented governance model during pre-1.0
development. Maintainers merge changes, manage releases, and decide compatibility
policy. Consequential security and architecture changes require a public ADR and at
least one review from a maintainer who did not author the change.

The project will add a technical steering committee after three independent active
maintainers and two production adopters exist. Until then, maintainer decisions and
their rationale are recorded in issues, pull requests, and ADRs.

No employer or vendor receives private product authority through a connector,
provider, or sponsorship relationship. Security embargoes are the only routine
exception to public technical discussion.
